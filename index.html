Put Pen Pal on your home screen first
  1. Tap the Share button in Safari (the square with the arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Pen Pal from your home screen and set up there.

Notifications only work from the home-screen app, and the home-screen app keeps its own login, so anything you set up here in Safari won't carry over.
